# Known PCH beta issues

**Experimental Tour** can stretch out or stall in traffic. The group can lose the player or stop in the travel lane instead of lining up at a pullout. Tour starts at the lower lot and disables challenges.

**Standard** starts at estate parking and supports distance challenges. Use it on your first Drive.

The California Plates release passed a Medium / Standard Sandbox Drive with the standalone Map Tool on Windows. The driver verified a traffic car and bus. After quitting, all 26 personal-save files matched the pre-Drive SHA-256 hashes.
